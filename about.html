Our Story

Coimbatore's Premier Wedding Photography Studio

Nuptial Studio is a premier wedding photography and cinematography studio based in Coimbatore. Founded with a passionate vision to craft timeless, emotional wedding stories across Tamil Nadu and South India.

Equipped with full-frame Sony FX3 & A7IV cinema cameras, Prime G-Master lenses, DJI 4K Drones, and professional studio lighting setups, our crew captures every priceless tear, warm embrace, and joyful dance step in razor-sharp 4K clarity.
