Privacy Policy

Your privacy and sacred family memories are safe with Nuptial Studio.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit inquiry forms, book photography sessions, or contact us via WhatsApp, we collect basic contact details including your full name, email address, phone number, wedding/event dates, and venue location. This information is strictly used to prepare quotations, communicate schedule updates, and plan your photography assignments.

2. Photograph & Video Media Usage

All raw and edited wedding photographs and 4K cinema footage captured during your events are stored on encrypted, redundant high-speed storage drives. Nuptial Studio respects client privacy preferences. If you prefer your wedding media not to be displayed in our online portfolio or social media highlights, please notify us during contract signing.

3. Data Security & Storage

We maintain industry-standard security protocols to protect your personal information and media against unauthorized access or disclosure. We retain edited photos in cloud storage backup for up to 12 months following event delivery.

4. Third-Party Services

We never sell or rent client data to third parties. Data is shared exclusively with certified photo album printers (such as Canvera) solely for printing your selected photobooks.
