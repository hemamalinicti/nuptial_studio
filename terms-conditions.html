Terms & Conditions

Transparent terms to ensure a smooth and memorable photography experience.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Booking & Advance Payment

Dates are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis upon receipt of a 30% advance booking deposit. The remaining balance is payable as per the mutually agreed milestone schedule (50% on event completion and 20% prior to final album/video delivery).

2. Delivery Timelines

• Teaser / Highlights: Delivered within 7–10 business days for social media sharing.
• Edited Photos: High-resolution digital copies delivered within 3–4 weeks.
• Canvera Photobooks: Handcrafted albums are dispatched within 3–4 weeks post client photo selection and layout approval.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellation

In case of unforeseen event date adjustments, Nuptial Studio will accommodate new dates subject to crew availability at no additional charge. For cancellations made at least 30 days prior to the event, advance payments are eligible for credit toward future studio bookings.

4. Travel & Outstation Shoot Logistics

For destination shoots and events outside Coimbatore district, round-trip travel expenses and local crew accommodation are arranged or reimbursed by the client as specified in the booking quote.

5. Creative Rights & Copyright

Clients receive full personal reproduction rights for all delivered high-resolution images and films. Nuptial Studio reserves standard artist copyright for studio exhibition and portfolio showcase unless explicitly requested otherwise in writing.
